This site has two streams, and the split is about length and obligation rather than importance.

**Notes** are short. A fragment, something I learned today, a single idea stated once and left alone. A note does not owe you an introduction, a motivation, or a conclusion. Some will be three sentences. Some will be a definition I got tired of looking up, written down so I stop looking it up. They are not drafts of posts; most of them will never grow.

**Posts** are essays. They have an argument, they build, and they take a position I have to defend for more than a paragraph. They cost considerably more to write, which is why there are fewer of them.

The rule I am using: if a thing needs sections, it wants to be a post. If it needs a heading, it has probably already decided.

Both streams get dated and corrected rather than quietly edited. A note that turns out to be wrong is more useful with the correction attached than deleted.
